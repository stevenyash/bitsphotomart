## Introduction

Bits Photo Mart is Kenya's premier creative media network that connects organizations with verified photographers, videographers, and documentary storytellers across all 47 counties. Our platform provides:

- **Photography & Videography Services** - Professional coverage for events, corporate functions, NGO fieldwork, and more
- **Documentary Production** - Full-service documentary creation from concept to delivery
- **Stock Media Library** - Authentic African photos and videos for purchase
- **Corporate Packages** - Retainer plans for organizations needing consistent content

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit [Bits Photo Mart](https://bitsphotomart.com)
2. Click **"Register"** in the navigation menu
3. Choose your account type:
   - **I'm a Client** - If you need photography services
   - **I'm a Photographer** - If you want to join our creative network

### Account Types

#### Client Account
- Browse and purchase stock photos/videos
- Request photography services
- Track your orders and requests
- Manage your collections

#### Photographer Account
- Upload and sell your photos/videos
- Receive job invitations
- Track earnings and withdrawals
- Manage your portfolio

## For Customers

### Browsing the Stock Library

1. Navigate to **Stock Library** from the main menu
2. Use category filters to find specific content:
   - Business & Workspaces
   - African Urban Life
   - Rural Life & Culture
   - Families & People
   - Agriculture & Food Production
   - Travel, Nature & Wildlife
   - Aerial/Drone Footage
   - Events, Celebrations & Community
   - Technology & Innovation
   - Education & Learning

3. Use the search bar or AI search to find specific content
4. Click on any image to view details, photographer info, and pricing

### Purchasing Stock Photos/Videos

1. Find the photo/video you want
2. Click **"Add to Cart"**
3. Continue browsing or click **"Checkout"**
4. Review your cart and proceed to payment
5. Choose payment method (M-Pesa, Stripe, or PayPal)
6. Complete payment and receive download link

### Requesting Photography Services

1. Navigate to **Services** or click **"Request Coverage"**
2. Select service type:
   - Event Coverage
   - Documentary Production
   - Custom Shoot
3. Fill in details:
   - Event title and description
   - Date and location
   - County
   - Budget range
4. Submit your request
5. Receive quotes from verified photographers
6. Accept a quote and confirm booking

### Managing Your Account

- **My Purchases** - View and download purchased media
- **My Collections** - Organize saved items into collections
- **My Requests** - Track service requests and quotes
- **Profile Settings** - Update personal information and preferences

## For Photographers

### Joining Our Network

1. Register as a **Photographer**
2. Complete your profile:
   - Personal information
   - Professional details (skills, experience, equipment)
   - Portfolio samples
   - Payment details (M-Pesa, PayPal, or bank account)
3. Submit for review
4. Once approved, start uploading and receiving jobs

### Uploading Stock Media

1. Log in to your **Photographer Dashboard**
2. Navigate to **Stock Media Management**
3. Upload photos/videos with:
   - Title and description
   - Category selection
   - Tags for searchability
   - Pricing
4. Submit for admin review
5. Once approved, your media is available for purchase

### Responding to Job Requests

1. Check **Available Jobs** in your dashboard
2. View job details and requirements
3. Submit your quote with:
   - Proposed price
   - Timeline
   - Additional notes
4. If selected, complete the job and submit work
5. Receive payment upon completion

### Withdrawing Earnings

1. Go to **Earnings** in your dashboard
2. View total earnings and available balance
3. Click **"Request Withdrawal"**
4. Select payment method
5. Enter amount and confirm
6. Funds are processed within 5-7 business days

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: What is Bits Photo Mart?**
A: Bits Photo Mart is a digital marketplace connecting organizations with verified photographers, videographers, and documentary producers across Kenya.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email us at info@bitsphotomart.com or use the contact form on our website.

### For Customers

**Q: Can I use purchased photos for commercial purposes?**
A: Standard license covers personal and commercial use. For extended commercial use, purchase an extended license or request a quote.

**Q: How long does it take to get a quote for photography services?**
A: Quotes typically arrive within 24-48 hours of submitting a request.

**Q: Can I cancel a service request?**
A: Yes, you can cancel before accepting a quote. Once a photographer is assigned, please contact support.

### For Photographers

**Q: How do I become a verified photographer?**
A: Complete your profile, submit sample work, and wait for admin approval. Typically takes 2-3 business days.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payouts are processed weekly. Withdrawal requests are processed within 5-7 business days.

**Q: What percentage do photographers earn?**
A: Photographers earn 50-60% commission per download.

### Technical

**Q: What file formats are supported for uploads?**
A: Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP. Videos: MP4, MOV (up to 4K resolution).

**Q: What's the maximum file size?**
A: Photos: 50MB per file. Videos: 2GB per file.

## Payment Issues & Manual Verification

### What to Do If M-Pesa Payment Fails

Sometimes network issues can cause M-Pesa payments to go through on your phone but not register on our system. Here&apos;s what to do:

#### 1. Check Payment Status
- Go to your Orders/Purchases page
- Find the pending order
- Click **&quot;Check Payment Status&quot;** to see if the payment has been processed

#### 2. Request Manual Verification
If you&apos;ve made payment but the status shows as &quot;PENDING&quot;:

1. Click **&quot;Request Manual Verification&quot;** on the order
2. Enter your M-Pesa transaction number (if prompted)
3. Our Finance Team will verify the payment with Safaricom
4. You&apos;ll receive a notification once verified (usually within 24 hours)

#### 3. What Happens Next
- **If verified**: Your order will be marked as &quot;PAID&quot; and you can download your photos
- **If rejected**: You&apos;ll be notified and may need to retry the payment

### Finance Manager Role

For organizations with high volume transactions, we offer a **Finance Manager** role that can:
- View all pending payment verifications
- Manually verify or reject payments
- Access payment history and reports
- Manage refund requests

Contact admin to request a Finance Manager account for your organization.
