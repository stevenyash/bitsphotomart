## Getting Started

### Join as a Photographer
1. Go to **[Register](/register?role=photographer)**
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (for M-Pesa payments)
   - Password
3. Submit your application
4. Wait for approval (usually 24-48 hours)

### What You'll Need
- ✅ Quality portfolio photos
- ✅ Valid ID or business registration
- ✅ M-Pesa or PayPal account for payouts
- ✅ Good quality images (at least 2000px wide)

## Uploading Photos

### Photo Requirements
| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Minimum Size | 2000px on longest side |
| Format | JPEG, PNG |
| File Size | Max 25MB per photo |
| Quality | High quality, properly exposed |
| Content | Original work only |

### How to Upload
1. Go to **[Photographer Portal](/photographer)**
2. Click **Upload Photos**
3. Select files from your computer
4. Fill in details for each photo:
   - **Title** - Descriptive name
   - **Description** - What's in the photo
   - **Category** - Choose appropriate category
5. Click **Upload**
6. Wait for moderation approval

### Photo Categories
- Events & Weddings
- Corporate & Business
- Portrait & People
- Product & Commercial
- Nature & Landscapes
- Urban & Architecture
- Food & Lifestyle
- Stock & Documentary

## Pricing Your Work

### How Pricing Works
- You upload your photos for BPM to review
- BPM will select and purchase photos within **7 days** of upload
- If a photo is not purchased within 7 days, it automatically disappears from the platform
- **Maximum 50 photos** can be uploaded at once
- BPM offers between **KES 50 - KES 1,000** per photo

### Your Earnings
- You receive payment directly from BPM
- Payments are processed via M-Pesa or bank transfer
- No public marketplace sales - BPM is your only buyer

### Tips
- Upload high-quality, original photos
- Ensure proper lighting and composition
- Photos should be at least 2000px wide

## Getting Paid

### Payout Methods
- 💰 **M-Pesa** - Instant to Kenyan numbers
- 🌍 **PayPal** - International photographers

### Requesting a Payout
1. Go to **[Dashboard](/dashboard)**
2. Click **Earnings** or **Payouts**
3. Check your balance (minimum KES 1,000)
4. Click **Request Payout**
5. Select payment method
6. Confirm

### Payout Schedule
- Requests processed within **7 business days**
- Payments sent via M-Pesa or PayPal
