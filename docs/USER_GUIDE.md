# Bits Photo Mart - User Guide

Welcome to Bits Photo Mart! This guide helps you get started with our platform quickly.

---

## Book Our Services

Need professional photography or videography? Here's how:

1. Visit [Services](/services)
2. Choose your service type (Photography, Videography, Drone, Documentary)
3. Click "Request Coverage" or "Request a Quote"
4. Fill in your requirements
5. Submit and wait for a quote

---

## Our BPM Pricing

- **Photos**: from 500 KES
- **HD Clips**: from 1,000 KES
- **4K Clips**: from 2,500 KES

---

## Navigate to Gallery

1. Go to [Gallery](/gallery)
2. Browse photos and videos
3. Click on any item to view details

---

## Purchasing Photos/Videos

1. Click on any photo to view details
2. Select license type (Standard or Extended)
3. Add to Cart or Buy Now
4. Proceed to Checkout
5. Choose payment method (M-Pesa, Stripe, or PayPal)
6. Complete payment
7. Download from your Dashboard

## Payment Methods

### M-Pesa (Kenya)

1. Select M-Pesa at checkout
2. Enter your phone number
3. Receive STK push on your phone
4. Enter PIN to confirm
5. Payment processed instantly

### Stripe (International)

1. Select Stripe at checkout
2. Enter card details
3. Complete 3D secure verification
4. Payment processed securely

### PayPal

1. Select PayPal at checkout
2. Login to your PayPal account
3. Confirm payment
4. Redirected back to complete order

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Can't login | Check email/password, try "Forgot Password" |
| Payment failed | Verify account details, try alternative payment |
| Photos not showing | Check if approved by admin |
| Can't upload | Ensure file format is supported (JPEG, PNG, MP4) |
| Payout delayed | Contact support with transaction ID |
