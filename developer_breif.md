BPM BRIEF
Initiative -BITS PHOTO-MART
1.0	Idea
To establish a photography, documentaries and stock footage platform that brings together artistic creators (photographers) from across the country. Besides covering events, we shall help clients to take n share video clips and photos of scenic and memorable sceneries, their wares and undertakings and deposit/place them in our system for sale -where we shall either buy these right from their dashboards or have them all in one collection point; sell and share proceeds on an agreed formula (e.g. 60/40).
2.0	Modus Operandi
The platform shall feature the following attributes/services:
•	Photographer Registration -We shall attract credible photographers to  register/subscribe on our platform for assignment of work as requested by customers within their locality. Once registered, they will get a dashboard on our platform. Once we receive a request form a prospect, we shall ask our subscribed photographers within the locale to bid for the job. This process shall entail them sharing their details for vetting and approval...........
•	Photos on our platform (Bits Photo Mart) shall be grouped by theme (and placed into sort of a catalogue) such that search by clients becomes easy. We shall then have a sort of n optimization engine on our platform to search for products/services already available -by theme
•	Drone & photos services -for event, for placement on Mini-photo shoot events, documentaries, etc. We shall source and partner with photographers across the cities to ensure we can provide these services all over the place at good rates (We shall advertise and provide these services anywhere in the country). Clientele shall get into our platform and fill a Service Request Form with personal details, a description of the exact service they need and submit for our next action -once submitted a popup shall be generated into our Admin area to notify BPM teams of the requests. Our teams shall then analyse the request and give appropriate response asap -this will entail contacting our satellite photographers (within that locality) to give their quick quotes (sending SMS asking them to log in and respond)
•	Sale of Photos -We shall retain a gallery of nice photos -stock footage; taken across the country (by us and our team of photographers scattered across the country) -all for sale. This will entail buying good photos posted on our website (Submit your photo for sale -where such photos posted will last for only three days for us to determine if to grant an offer and buy it then it automatically gets deleted/timed out -to avoid overuse of space)
•	Photo Cropping, overlay & embossing services -We shall offer these services on best scenic photos in our repository as selected by the client. This service will equally entail framing for portraits and rollups for individuals, teams, etc.
•	Documentaries (Fun & Convenience Documentaries) -done by our teams using a client’s referred photos and video clips carefully taken by our teams -animated with Mr Smart sort of artwork…
•	Your Photo-ed/curated story of the day -a client can book space at our ‘Story for the Day’ and have their tale/business featured as a top story for their prospects to view it at a glance
